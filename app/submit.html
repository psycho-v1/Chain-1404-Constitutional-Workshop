          Article: Governor

          Title: No silent parameter change

          Body: The Governor SHALL publish any change to quorum, delay, or treasury limit in a public proposal for at least seven days before it can execute.
        

      

      

        
Words that work

        
Start with SHALL or SHALL NOT. Name the actor. Name the action. Avoid slogans.
