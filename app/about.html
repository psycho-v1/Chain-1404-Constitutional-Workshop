About the workshop

The Constitutional Workshop is a public record of how the Chain 1404 community wants a DAO to be formed. Seats are equal. Clauses are public. Sealing is permissionless once the bar is met.

It is not an official product of the BlockDAG company. It does not custody funds. The seat bond is sent to a burn address.
