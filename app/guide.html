For people who are not developers

How to take part

You do not need to write code. You need a phone or computer, a little BDAG for fees, and the seat bond (currently 1 BDAG, burned).

Do these four things, in order

1
Open this site in your wallet browser

On a phone open the MetaMask app → Browser and paste this site. On a computer use Chrome or Brave with the MetaMask extension. Ordinary Safari or Chrome on a phone cannot sign.

2
Connect, then get a seat

Tap Connect wallet. Approve Chain 1404 if asked. Go to Get a seat and tap Attest. Your wallet will send the seat bond. That amount is destroyed on purpose so extra fake seats cost something.

Example: wallet 0x2AD7…02A4 pays 1 BDAG, receives seat #1, and can now write and vote.
3
Write one clear rule

Open Write a clause. Pick an article. Keep the title short. Write the body as a rule the future DAO must follow.

Good title: Treasurer cannot move funds alone
Good body: The DAO SHALL require two independent signatures before any treasury transfer above 1,000 BDAG.
Weak body: “Be honest.” That cannot be sealed as a rule. Say who must do what.
4
Vote, wait three days, seal

Open the clause in the ledger. Tap Yea or Nay once. After three days, if at least half of the seats that existed when it was posted have voted, and yea is more than nay, anyone may tap Seal. Sealed text moves into the living draft.

The five articles
  • Membership — who may sit in a later DAO.
  • Governor — how proposals and vetoes should work.
  • Scope — what the DAO is allowed to govern.
  • Infrastructure — chains, RPCs, tools, records.
  • Amendment — how this constitution may change later.
Simple rules that surprise people
  • The bond does not come back if you leave.
  • Leave once and this wallet cannot sit again.
  • You may keep at most 5 unsealed clauses of your own.
  • You can only vote on clauses posted after you sat.
  • Quorum is 50% of seats that existed at post time, not 50% of whoever showed up.
