# Privacy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

## What the interface sees

The static pages run in your browser. They talk to the RPC endpoints listed in `launch.json` and, if you choose, to an injected wallet. The pages do not open an account form and do not ask for a name, email, phone, or seed.

## What the chain sees

A public blockchain stores the address you use, the seat bond movement, clause text, and votes. That data is worldwide and permanent. Treat every clause as a press release.

## Cookies and accounts

The interface does not set an authentication cookie and does not keep a member directory off-chain. Local storage, if the browser creates any, is under your control.

## Third parties

Wallet software, RPC operators, and content delivery networks for the script libraries have their own logs. This repository does not control those logs.

## Children

The record is not directed at children. Do not attest a seat for a minor.

## Requests

Because the record is a public chain, nobody can honour a “delete my clause” request against sealed or even open storage. Revoking a seat stops future marks. It does not erase history.
