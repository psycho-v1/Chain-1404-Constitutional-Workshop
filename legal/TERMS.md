# Terms of Use

Last updated: 8 September 2026

These terms govern your use of the Constitutional Workshop interface and the on-chain formation record it reads. By connecting a wallet or submitting a transaction you accept them.

## 1. What this is

The Workshop is a public formation record. Members may attest a seat, post a clause, mark a clause, and seal a clause when the contract’s own rules allow it. The interface is a viewer and a signing helper. The contract is the record.

## 2. What this is not

This project is not a bank, exchange, broker, investment club, or legal person. It does not take custody of BDAG except the seat bond, which the contract burns to a fixed sink. It does not promise a token price, a listing, a refund, or a later treasury.

## 3. Your wallet

You act from an address you control. You are responsible for the device, the seed, the gas, and the network you select. A mistaken network, a phishing page, or a leaked key is your loss.

## 4. Seats and bonds

Attestation sends the seat bond to a burn address. The bond does not return if you revoke, if you dislike an outcome, or if you stop using the interface. A vacated address cannot sit again on the same record.

## 5. Speech you post

Clauses you submit are public and durable. Do not post personal data you are not willing to see copied. Do not post content that is unlawful in your jurisdiction. The interface operators — if any exist — are not editors of the chain.

## 6. No fiduciary

Nobody behind this repository is your adviser, agent, or fiduciary. Sealed text is a community artefact. It does not create a partnership among seats.

## 7. Experimental software

Smart contracts and browsers fail. Read the audit. Do not size a seat bond as money you cannot burn. Past events on Chain 1404 are context, not a warranty.

## 8. Local law

You must not use the record where doing so is illegal for you. You are solely responsible for taxes and filings that your own activity creates.

## 9. Changes

The interface text may change. Sealed clauses on an already published record do not change. If a later interface disagrees with the contract, the contract wins.

## 10. Contact

There is no support desk implied by these terms. Disputes about clause meaning are for the community that wrote them, not for this file.
