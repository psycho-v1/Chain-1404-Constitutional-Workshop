# Community Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

## Purpose

The Workshop exists so holders and users of community Chain 1404 can write, in public, how a later DAO must be formed. It is a workshop, not the DAO.

## Equality

During formation, seats are equal. Do not campaign for a token-weighted override of an unsealed census. Do not sell seats. The contract already refuses transfers; social markets around them are still a violation of this policy.

## Honesty of text

Write one rule per clause. Use language a later Governor could execute without a blog post. Do not paste rumours as SHALL-text. Rationale belongs in conversation; the body field is the rule.

## Conduct

Doxxing, intimidation, and impersonation of other seats are out of bounds. So is using the clause body to stash malware, personal dossiers, or copyrighted dumps.

## Sealing

Anyone may call seal when the contract says a clause is ready. Do not treat a failed seal as censorship. Quorum and majority are the gate.

## Scope lock

Do not use this record to move a treasury, to invent a claim portal, or to declare a single private RPC as “the network.” Those acts belong nowhere in v1.

## Interface operators

If someone hosts a copy of these pages, they host a viewer. They do not become the Governor, the company, or a moderator of sealed storage.
